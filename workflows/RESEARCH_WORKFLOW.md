# RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md
Version: 0.1-bootstrap

## Research pipeline

Scout → Evidence Store → Evidence Audit → Synthesis → Opportunity Analysis → Concept Generation → Red Team → Human Gate

Rules:
- scouts collect evidence and provenance; they do not decide the product
- analysts distinguish facts, user sentiment, inference, and hypothesis
- competitor research uses one shared schema
- current products and market facts must be verified from fresh sources
- legal/licensing claims require authoritative sources
- no implementation task is created from research until an approved product/design gate authorizes it

## Required early streams
1. Direct football management competitors
2. Adjacent football products
3. Player-review/community mining
4. Cross-genre retention/progression mechanics
5. Rights/licensing/data-source research
6. UI/UX reference research
7. Market gap / white-space analysis

## Output location
Research outputs go under `reports/research/` or later approved structured evidence stores.
