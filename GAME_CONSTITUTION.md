# GAME_CONSTITUTION.md
Version: 0.1-bootstrap
Status: DRAFT — DESIGN MODE
Authority: MASTER.md

This file records non-negotiable product principles before implementation.

1. REAL FOOTBALL FIRST.
2. SIMULATION != GAME.
3. Official modes must not invent real players, clubs, managers, competitions, transfers, or historical facts.
4. Player-facing gameplay must not degrade into admin forms, raw tables, JSON, or debug interfaces.
5. No pay-to-win competitive advantage.
6. Player decisions must have legible consequences.
7. Production UI requires an approved design system and player-experience validation.
8. AI-generated text may present verified football facts but may not invent them.
9. Product depth and accessibility must be balanced intentionally, not accidentally.
10. Builders may not reinterpret frozen product rules after SPEC FREEZE.

This draft is not SPEC FREEZE. It may be refined during approved DESIGN tasks.
